Put these PNG files in this folder (names can be changed in CONFIG at the top of game.js).
All aircraft pictures: TOP view, NOSE POINTING UP, transparent background.

akasa-b737.png   Your aircraft          300 x 330 px
air-india.png    Oncoming Air India     300 x 330 px   (game turns it to face you)
indigo.png       Oncoming IndiGo        300 x 330 px   (game turns it to face you)
background.png   Background             900 x 1600 px  tall, top edge must join bottom edge (seamless)
result.png       Finish / crash popup   600 x 400 px

Side-view aircraft pictures (nose pointing right)? Set planeImageFacing and
obstacleImageFacing to "right" in game.js and they will be turned automatically.
